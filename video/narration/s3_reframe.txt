Weave reframes version control as event sourcing. Git stores snapshots and diffs. Weave stores a causal intent log as the single source of truth — and the repository is just a materialized view, a fold over intents. The atomic unit isn't a diff, it's an intent: the goal, the plan, the operations, the verification, and the reasoning behind the change. Branches become streams — agents publish intents, and the system continuously integrates. And conflicts? We don't do text conflicts anymore. Weave merges at the semantic level, and when two changes genuinely interact, the agents negotiate — with the whole discussion recorded as a versioned decision.
